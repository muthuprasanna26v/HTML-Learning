Basic Audio

Audio Attributes

  • controls - Shows audio controls (play, pause, volume)
  • autoplay - Starts playing automatically (use with caution)
  • loop - Replays audio when finished
  • muted - Audio starts muted
  • preload - How the browser should load the audio

Audio with Multiple Sources

Best Practices

Always provide text alternatives for audio content for accessibility.

Use autoplay sparingly as it can be disruptive to users.
