Open Graph Meta Tags

What are Open Graph Tags?

Open Graph tags control how your content appears when shared on social media platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn, and Twitter.

Essential Open Graph Tags

  • og:title - The title of your content
  • og:description - A description of your content
  • og:image - The URL of an image for your content
  • og:url - The canonical URL of your page
  • og:type - The type of your content (website, article, etc.)

Twitter Cards

Twitter has its own set of meta tags called Twitter Cards for controlling how content appears on Twitter.

Best Practices

  • Use high-quality images (1200x630px recommended)
  • Write compelling titles and descriptions
  • Use absolute URLs for images
  • Test your tags using social media debugging tools
